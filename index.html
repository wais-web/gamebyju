PICK THE LOCK

Tumblers surface around the lock and narrow as they age until there's nothing left to hit. Click when the pick crosses one — it's +1,000, the tumbler pops, and the pick reverses direction.
Gold pays points. Blue pays points and +1.5s — blue is the only thing that refills the clock.
Consecutive picks build a chain — every 3 raises your multiplier, up to ×10. A whiff costs no time but drags the pick and resets the chain to zero. Letting a tumbler expire untouched is safe.
The longer you last, the more tumblers come at once.
